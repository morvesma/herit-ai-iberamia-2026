ABOUT THE WORKSHOP

Artificial intelligence, cultural heritage and responsible innovation

HERIT-AI brings together researchers, cultural institutions, museums, archives, digital-humanities scholars and technology practitioners to discuss trustworthy, explainable and inclusive applications of artificial intelligence for cultural heritage.

CALL FOR PAPERS

Share your research and ideas

We invite contributions addressing the scientific, technological, ethical and social challenges of applying AI to tangible and intangible cultural heritage.

Full call for papers — coming soon

Topics of interest

  • Trustworthy, explainable and responsible AI
  • NLP, LLMs and historical documents
  • Knowledge representation and knowledge graphs
  • Generative AI and digital storytelling
  • Museums, archives and digital heritage
  • Misinformation and historical narratives
